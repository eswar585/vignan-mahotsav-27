THE SAGA OF EXCELLENCE

The Arc of Becoming

Now in its landmark 20th Edition, VIGNAN MAHOTSAV has stood as South India's grandest national cultural and sports summit. With over 90+ competitive events spanning Performing Arts, Visual Arts, Literary Declamations, High-Octane Esports, Combat Robotics, and Track & Field, Mahotsav is where collegiate talent transforms into legendary triumph.

🎭

Culturals Spectrum

Solo & Group Classical Dance, Battle of the Bands, Fashion Runway, Theatrics, Live Canvas Painting, Photography, and Literary Declamations.

Fee: ₹ 250 (All Cultural Events Included)
⚡

RoboGames & Gaming

30kg Heavyweight RoboWars, Line Following Robots, Valorant LAN Apex Cup, BGMI Showdown, and FIFA 24 Console Championships.

Cash Prizes Worth ₹ 2,50,000+

Sports & Para-Athletics

Olympic 400m Synthetic Track & Field, Basketball, Volleyball, Badminton, Table Tennis, and inclusive Para-Sports & Boccia championships.

Men: ₹ 350 | Women: ₹ 250
